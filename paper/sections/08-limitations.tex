\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\label{sec:notrun}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{What is counted, and on which panel.} The GitHub panel is the repositories still public in
  September 2026 with complete histories, and the endpoint reports surviving stars; an npm download is a
  tarball request, including mirrors and automated clients, a directional indicator of adoption and not a
  count of users; a 311 request is a complaint or inquiry about a city service, not a measure of the
  condition it reports. The NYC 311 targets were read once, 47 days after the last target day, with no check for later revision.
\item \emph{Training contamination is unresolved.} No author-disclosed complete-corpus training cutoff
  exists for any of the three models; the targets postdate the pinned TimesFM 2.5 revision but are not shown
  to postdate the pinned Chronos-2 or TimesFM 3 revisions, and a later window on familiar entities does not
  establish generalization to unseen ones \citep{moghadasi2026, qiao2026, li2026}. The controls that would
  separate familiarity from skill, Study 1's registered follow-up cohort and the October holdout, have not
  run.
\item \emph{Three origins in one summer, horizons that share targets.} Inference is conditional on three fixed cutoffs; the 7-day blocks are prefixes of the 30-day blocks, so the cells of a family are not independent; the bootstrap captures between-series variation only and its intervals are pointwise.
\item \emph{Three models from two families, one interface.} No other model was run, and none released since the pins \citep{exaone2026}. Every
  conclusion about occurrence is a conclusion about nine deciles completed into a count law; a model whose
  output is a mixture, a sample path or Chronos-2's own 21 quantile levels would not meet the floor of
  Section~\ref{sec:design} in the same place.
\item \emph{One series carries every npm mean.} With equal series weights on the unscaled score, as
  registered, every npm mean is a statement about \texttt{aggregate-error}, at 7.6 million downloads a day;
  without it the six hurdle-against-control means fall from 90.24 to 253.9 to 0.6334 to 1.599. The context-scaled score is the registered scale-free secondary (Appendix~\ref{app:s2tables}). The npm very sparse stratum holds nine series, so Study
  2 says nothing about npm series that are zero nine days in ten.
\item \emph{Three zero days in the npm targets.} The download API served 0 for all 209 series on
  2026-07-12 and 2026-08-14 and 0 for the largest series on 2026-06-25, between days of millions; npm's own
  records report no outage or backfill for them, and a public thread records two earlier episodes in which
  whole days read 0 for nearly every package and were later restored \citep{npm-community-150808}. Item 6 of
  the ledger keeps the registered analysis as the confirmatory one; the post-hoc sensitivity changes no
  conclusion without all three days and one row, E06, without the two panel-wide days; nothing here
  verifies that they are gaps.
\item \emph{The signed-rank test with many exact ties, and the direction rule.} The registered inference
  omits zero differences and reports $p = 1$ when all are zero; with 72 to 88 ties a very sparse NYC 311 test
  runs on 12 to 28 series, its location reading needs symmetry, which these differences do not have, and it is
  not a test of the mean. The registered direction is the median's sign, and a median of exactly zero has
  none; the side the rank sums favor is a reading beside it that the registration does not make.
\item \emph{Registered and not run.} Study 1's follow-up cohort, the October holdout H01 to H18, Study 2's
  deferred tests E19 to E54 on NYC 311 and Wikipedia pageviews, and a November prospective round that was
  drafted and never appended have no result, and Study 3's family replaces none of them. The grid has no
  overdispersed cell; no Markov occurrence estimate was tested; and the terms under which the NYC 311 counts
  may be reused rest on the city website's terms of use, read on 2026-10-17 and recorded, the Open Data Law
  the program names not having been read.
\end{itemize}
