\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\label{sec:notrun}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{What is counted, and on which panel.} The GitHub panel is the repositories still public in
  September 2026 with complete histories, and the endpoint reports surviving stars. An npm download is a
  tarball request, including mirrors and automated clients, a directional indicator of adoption and not a
  count of users. A 311 request is a complaint or inquiry about a city service, not a measure of the condition
  it reports. On NYC 311 the targets were read once, 47 days after the last target day, with no check for
  later revision. Each result is therefore a statement about the counts as served, not about the people or the
  conditions behind them.
\item \emph{Training contamination is unresolved.} No author-disclosed complete-corpus training cutoff exists
  for any of the three models. The targets postdate the pinned TimesFM 2.5 revision but are not shown to
  postdate the pinned Chronos-2 or TimesFM 3 revisions, and a later window on familiar entities does not
  establish generalization to unseen ones \citep{moghadasi2026, qiao2026, li2026}. Study 1's registered
  follow-up cohort and the October holdout, the controls that would separate familiarity from skill, have not
  run. Until they do, it cannot be ruled out that part of the models' gain is familiarity rather than skill.
\item \emph{Three origins in one summer, horizons that share targets.} Inference is conditional on three fixed
  cutoffs. Because the 7-day blocks are prefixes of the 30-day blocks, the cells of a family are not
  independent. The bootstrap captures between-series variation only, and its intervals are pointwise.
  Consequently, the results may not hold in another season, and agreement between the two horizons counts for
  less than two independent findings would.
\item \emph{Three models from two families, one interface.} No other model was run, and none released since
  the pins \citep{exaone2026}. Every conclusion about occurrence is a conclusion about nine deciles completed
  into a count law. A model whose output is a mixture, a sample path or Chronos-2's own 21 quantile levels
  would not meet the floor of Section~\ref{sec:design} in the same place. For such a model the very sparse
  question may well be answerable.
\item \emph{One series carries every npm mean.} With equal series weights on the unscaled score, as
  registered, every npm mean is a statement about \texttt{aggregate-error}, at 7.6 million downloads a day.
  Without it the six hurdle-against-control means fall from 90.24 to 253.9 to 0.6334 to 1.599. In other words,
  the npm means describe one package, and the medians, counts and rank tests describe the typical one. The
  context-scaled score is the registered scale-free secondary (Appendix~\ref{app:s2tables}). Only nine series
  qualify for the npm very sparse stratum, so Study 2 says nothing about npm series that are zero nine days in
  ten.
\item \emph{Three zero days in the npm targets.} The download API served 0 for all 209 series on 2026-07-12
  and 2026-08-14 and 0 for the largest series on 2026-06-25, between days of millions. However, npm's own
  records report no outage or backfill for them, and a public thread records two earlier episodes in which
  whole days read 0 for nearly every package and were later restored \citep{npm-community-150808}. Item 6 of
  the ledger keeps the registered analysis as the confirmatory one. The post-hoc sensitivity changes no
  conclusion without all three days and one row, E06, without the two panel-wide days. Nothing here verifies
  that they are gaps. In either case, the answer on occurrence does not depend on them.
\item \emph{The signed-rank test with many exact ties, and the direction rule.} The registered inference omits
  zero differences and reports $p = 1$ when all are zero. With 72 to 88 ties a very sparse NYC 311 test runs
  on 12 to 28 series. Its location reading needs symmetry, which these differences do not have, and it is not
  a test of the mean. The registered direction is the median's sign, and a median of exactly zero has none.
  Beside it, the side the rank sums favor is a reading that the registration does not make. A rejection with a
  median of exactly zero, like the three very sparse rejections against the empirical hurdle, therefore has no
  registered direction.
\item \emph{Registered and not run.} Study 1's follow-up cohort, the October holdout H01 to H18, Study 2's
  deferred tests E19 to E54 on NYC 311 and Wikipedia pageviews, and a November prospective round that was
  drafted and never appended have no result. Nor does Study 3's family replace any of them. Nothing here,
  then, speaks for Wikipedia pageviews or for the October and November windows. The grid has no overdispersed
  cell, and no Markov occurrence estimate was tested. Reuse of the NYC 311 counts rests on the city website's
  terms of use, read on 2026-10-17 and recorded, but the Open Data Law the program names was not read.
\end{itemize}
