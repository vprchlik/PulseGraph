\paragraph{The run.} The external run started \tbd{} UTC at implementation commit \texttt{\tbd{}} after the
signed readiness and the gate, and completed in \tbd{} seconds with \tbd{} forecast rows (209 series, 3
origins, 2 horizons, 16 forecasts per origin), \tbd{} failed windows and \tbd{} resumes; the integrity
check recomputed every score and verified all 209 checkpoint hashes. The synthetic run started \tbd{} UTC at
commit \texttt{\tbd{}} and completed in \tbd{} seconds with \tbd{} rows over 540 series. The discrete CRPS
was recorded as unavailable for \tbd{} forecasts, all of them on the series named in
Section~\ref{sec:s2panel}, and is reported over the remaining denominator.

\paragraph{Occurrence replaced, positive law kept (E01, E04, E07, E10, E13, E16; S01, S03, S05).} Against its
own completed control, the hurdle variant's mean difference \dir{favors the hurdle / favors the control} in
\tbd{} of the six external cells, and its bootstrap interval excludes zero in \tbd{} of them; the hurdle
scores lower on \tbd{} to \tbd{} of the 209 series, every median difference is \dir{negative / positive},
and the signed-rank test rejects after adjustment in \tbd{} of the six cells, in each case on the side of
the \dir{hurdle / control}. On the synthetic grid the hurdle's mean difference against its own control
\dir{favors the hurdle / favors the control} for \tbd{} of the three models, with the interval excluding
zero for \tbd{}; the hurdle scores lower on \tbd{} to \tbd{} of the 30 replicates. The zero-day part of the
difference (the three exact accounting parts, reported with the full horizon denominator) is
\dir{negative / positive} in \tbd{} of the six external cells, from \tbd{} to \tbd{}, and the positive-day
parts sum to \tbd{} to \tbd{}; so the substitution \dir{lowers / raises} the zero-day loss and \dir{lowers
/ raises / leaves unchanged} the positive-day loss, and the net sign is as the mean says.

\paragraph{The model's positive law against a fixed count hurdle (E02, E05, E08, E11, E14, E17; S02, S04,
S06).} Against \hc{}, which shares the hurdle variant's occurrence probability and differs only in the
positive law, the hurdle variant's mean difference \dir{favors the variant / favors the count hurdle} in
\tbd{} of the six external cells, with the interval excluding zero in \tbd{}; the variant scores lower on
\tbd{} to \tbd{} of 209 series, the medians are \dir{negative / positive}, and the signed-rank test rejects
in \tbd{} cells on the side of the \dir{variant / count hurdle}. On the synthetic grid the same contrast
\dir{favors the variant / favors the count hurdle} for \tbd{} of three models, lower on \tbd{} to \tbd{}
of 30 replicates. Since the occurrence rule is common to both sides, this contrast isolates the positive
law: the foundation model's completed size forecast \dir{improves on / does not improve on} a shifted
Poisson fitted to the last 28 days.

\paragraph{The raw deciles against the local mean (E03, E06, E09, E12, E15, E18).} The Study 1 contrast,
repeated on npm: the raw native deciles' mean difference against \lm{} \dir{favors the model / favors the
reference} in \tbd{} of six cells, with the interval excluding zero in \tbd{}; the models score lower on
\tbd{} to \tbd{} of 209 series and the medians are \dir{negative / positive}. This \dir{agrees with /
differs from} the Study 1 pattern of a mean gain beside a typical-series loss.

\paragraph{Read together.} \tbd{} of the eighteen external tests and \tbd{} of the six synthetic tests reject
at $q = 0.05$ after one adjustment per family. The registered question has two parts and each is answered
by its own contrasts: replacing the occurrence probability \dir{helps / does not help / hurts} on \qs{}, and
the model's positive law \dir{beats / does not beat} a fixed count hurdle. The mean on npm is read with the
one dominant series in view (Section~\ref{sec:s2panel}); the secondary scaled \qs{}, the Brier score, the
CRPS over its available denominator, the PIT histograms and the per-stratum tables with $n = 9$ in the very
sparse stratum are in Appendix~\tbd{}, and add no test. Null, adverse, mixed and imprecise outcomes are
reported with the same care as a positive one.
