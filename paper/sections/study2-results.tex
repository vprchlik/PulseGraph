\section{Study 2: results}
\label{sec:s2results}

Tables~\ref{tab:s2ext} and~\ref{tab:s2syn} give the eighteen external and the six synthetic comparisons
with the columns of Tables~\ref{tab:lm} and~\ref{tab:ae}: the mean difference and its interval, the median
difference, the number of series (of 209) or replicate blocks (of 30) where the model variant's \qs{} is
lower, tied or higher, and the adjusted $p$-value. Differences are model variant minus reference, so negative
favors the variant. The two summaries, the mean and the typical series, are shown together as in Study 1,
because they disagree; for the external family the mean is read with Section~\ref{sec:s2panel} in view,
since one series carries every mean in Table~\ref{tab:s2ext}. Twelve of the eighteen external tests and
five of the six synthetic tests reject at $q = 0.05$ after one adjustment per family; of the twelve, ten
reject for the model variant and two against it, and all five synthetic rejections are against the hurdle
variant. Figures~\ref{fig:s2npm} and~\ref{fig:s2cells} show the per-series and per-cell differences behind
the two tables.

\paragraph{The runs.} The synthetic run started 2026-10-15 20:16 UTC at implementation commit
\texttt{3fc3411f7c683aaf98e6fad28b26aa9c92ab00b9}, after the signed readiness (approved 20:13 UTC, runtime
budget 2,400 seconds against an estimate of 1,539) and the gate, and completed its 540 series in one
invocation in 1,317 seconds (1,323 for the whole process) with 55,080 forecasts (540 series, 3 origins, 2
horizons, the 16 variants and the oracle), no failed window and no resume. The npm run started 2026-10-15
23:27 UTC at implementation commit \texttt{3057b16dbd2f9b218449352717ef4adabc2f72f7}, after the signed
readiness (approved 23:09 UTC, at most 300 target requests, a budget of 3,600 seconds against an estimate of
1,741) and the gate, which passed once before any target request and once more with the pulled signals and
their completeness document; it completed its 209 series in one invocation in 692 seconds (699 for the whole
process) with 20,064 forecasts (209 series, 3 origins, 2 horizons, 16 variants), no failed window and no
resume. Both ran on the same NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 SUPER (driver 610.88, CUDA 12.8, PyTorch 2.11.0) at
batch size one, with a peak reserved memory of 2,948,595,712 bytes, from the locally cached weights. The
targets, the 209 series' download ranges for 2026-06-03 to 2026-08-31, were requested once each from the
download-counts API between 23:14 and 23:26 UTC, 209 attempts and 209 answers, with no retry and no
failure; every answer lists each of the 90 days once with a nonnegative integer. The integrity recomputation
of the npm run verified all 209 checkpoint hashes and recomputed \qs{}, its three accounting parts, the
context-scaled \qs{} and, where a count law is saved, the occurrence Brier score for all 20,064 forecasts
from the saved deciles, targets and laws, with 0 disagreements; the recomputation of the synthetic run
verified all 540 checkpoint hashes and recomputed \qs{}, its parts and the scaled score for all 55,080
forecasts with a largest absolute difference of 0.0, and found every saved target and context mean equal to
the registered generator's series. The discrete CRPS was recorded as unavailable, under the addition of
2026-10-15, for 66 npm forecasts, all of them on \texttt{aggregate-error} (11 variants with a count law, 3
origins, 2 horizons); the other 13,728 forecasts with a count law are certified, and the 6,270 without one
(the raw deciles, \lm{} and \aets{}) have no CRPS by construction. On the synthetic grid every forecast with
a count law is certified.

\paragraph{Three zero days in the npm targets.} After the run was scored, the saved targets were found to
read 0 for every one of the 209 series on 2026-07-12 and 2026-08-14, days on which the panel's total is 0
between neighbours of 4,059,870 and 10,123,968 downloads, and 7,480,108 and 2,560,140; and 0 for
\texttt{aggregate-error} alone on 2026-06-25, between 9,475,033 and 7,909,626, when 92 of the 209 series
read 0 against 98 and 121 on the days either side. No day of the 275-day context reads 0 for more than 153
of the 209 series, and the largest series never reads below 1,109,682 there. The registered completeness
rule, fixed before any target was seen, verifies that every day is present once with a nonnegative integer
and does not test for a source-wide zero, so the three days entered the run as explicit zeros and were
scored as served. Each falls in one horizon-30 target block, one per origin, and in no horizon-7 block. npm's
own status history, trackers and changelog report no outage, gap or backfill for the three days; the status
page does not track download counts; and npm's community forum records two earlier episodes, in early 2025
and March 2026, in which whole days read 0 for nearly every package and were later restored. The dated
addition of 2026-10-16 to the protocol, item 6, made after the scores were seen, keeps the registered
analysis of the data as served as the confirmatory result for E01 to E18, reclassifies no day, replaces no
series and stops no domain, and requires a post-hoc sensitivity, reported at the end of this section, that
removes the days and re-scores the saved forecasts. Whether the days are gaps in the source is a reading
from npm's earlier episodes and from one third-party page, not something the source confirms.

\paragraph{Occurrence replaced, positive law kept (E01, E04, E07, E10, E13, E16; S01, S03, S05).} Against
its own completed control, the hurdle variant's mean difference is positive, favoring the control, in all six
external cells, from 90.24 to 253.9, with every interval above zero; the medians lie within 0.014 of zero,
three below it and three above; the hurdle scores lower on 81 to 121 of the 209 series; and the signed-rank
test rejects after adjustment in two of the six cells, TimesFM 3 and TimesFM 2.5 at horizon 30 (E10, with
the hurdle higher on 120 of 209 series and adjusted $p$ 0.00215; E16, higher on 107, adjusted $p$ 0.0276),
in both cases on the side of the control. The other four are not rejected, with adjusted $p$ from 0.203 to
0.628; not rejecting is not equivalence. No external comparison favors the hurdle variant. On the synthetic
grid the hurdle's mean difference against its own control favors the control for all three models, from
0.08599 to 0.08769, with every interval above zero and medians of 0.05554 to 0.06743; the hurdle scores
lower on 5 or 6 of the 30 replicate blocks; and all three tests reject, with adjusted $p$ from 0.00415 to
0.00676, against the hurdle. Table~\ref{tab:s2parts} splits each hurdle-minus-control difference into the
three exact accounting parts of Section~6, each over the full horizon denominator: in every one of the nine
cells the substitution lowers the zero-day loss and lowers the positive-day overprediction loss, and raises
the positive-day underprediction loss by more than both, so the net sign is the control's. That is the trade
the registered text allowed for, and it goes the same way on both studies. On the grid the hurdle variants'
zero-day parts are 0.3947 to 0.4294 against 0.5798 to 0.624 for their controls, and their underprediction
parts 1.316 to 1.39 against 1.029 to 1.085.

\paragraph{The model's positive law against a fixed count hurdle (E02, E05, E08, E11, E14, E17; S02, S04,
S06).} Against \hc{}, which shares the hurdle variant's occurrence probability and differs only in the
positive law, all six external tests reject in favor of the variant, with adjusted $p$ from
$2.66 \times 10^{-5}$ to $3.19 \times 10^{-25}$: the mean difference favors the variant in every cell, from
-5,090 to -6,918, with every interval below zero; the medians are negative, from -0.006349 to -0.0716; and
the variant scores lower on 115 to 161 of the 209 series. The occurrence Brier difference is exactly zero for
every series in these six rows, because the two sides use the same occurrence probability, so the gain is in
the positive law; the context-scaled differences run from -0.1182 to -0.1466 with every interval below zero.
On the synthetic grid the same contrast goes the other way or is not rejected: the Chronos-2 and TimesFM 2.5
hurdles score higher than the count hurdle, by 0.05211 and 0.06109 with intervals above zero (adjusted $p$
0.0111 and 0.00798, lower on 9 of 30 blocks each), and the TimesFM 3 hurdle differs by 0.0215 with an
interval that includes zero (adjusted $p$ 0.12, lower on 11 of 30). So the foundation model's completed size
forecast improves on a shifted Poisson fitted to the last 28 days on the npm panel and does not on the grid,
whose positive law is a shifted Poisson by construction.

\paragraph{The raw deciles against the local mean (E03, E06, E09, E12, E15, E18).} The Study 1 contrast,
repeated on npm: four of the six tests reject in favor of the model, Chronos-2 at horizon 30 (E06), TimesFM
3 at both horizons (E09, E12) and TimesFM 2.5 at horizon 30 (E18); Chronos-2 and TimesFM 2.5 at horizon 7
(E03, E15) are not rejected, with adjusted $p$ 0.596 and 0.746. The mean difference favors the model in all
six cells, from -2,884 to -5,109, with every interval below zero, and the models score lower on 84 to 125 of
the 209 series. The medians favor the reference at horizon 7 for Chronos-2 and TimesFM 2.5 (0.007856 and
0.01696) and the model in the other four cells (-0.002297 to -0.005016). This is the Study 1 pattern in
part: a mean gain carried by few series, beside a typical series that the local mean serves about as well,
here with the rank test on the model's side in four cells rather than on the reference's side in all nine.

\paragraph{The one series behind every npm mean.} In each of the eighteen rows of Table~\ref{tab:s2ext}
the series whose omission moves the mean most is \texttt{aggregate-error}, whose last 28 context days
average 7,637,136 downloads a day, and its paired difference has the sign of the row's mean in every row.
Without it the six hurdle-against-control means fall from 90.24 to 253.9 to 0.6334 to 1.599, and in the
twelve rows against \hc{} and \lm{} the five largest positive gains hold 0.995 to 0.998 of all positive
gain. The medians, the lower-tied-higher counts, the signed-rank tests and the per-stratum counts below are
rank-based and unaffected; the context-scaled \qs{} is the registered scale-free secondary. The mean paired
difference over 209 equally weighted series is, as registered, that series' difference divided by 209 plus a
remainder of a different order (Section~\ref{sec:s2panel}), and the reader should take every mean in the
external table as a statement about that series.

\paragraph{Secondary readings.} The strata, the context-scaled score, the Brier score, the oracle and the
per-cell differences are declared secondary and add no test; Appendix~\ref{app:s2tables} gives them in
full. In brief: by stratum, with $n = 9$ in the very sparse stratum, the hurdle variant scores lower than
its control on 32 to 43 of the 100 dense series, on 45 to 81 of the 100 sparse series and on 2 to 4 of the
9 very sparse series, and lower than \hc{} on 80 to 94 dense, 29 to 63 sparse and 4 of 9 very sparse
series. The context-scaled score favors the hurdle over its control at horizon 7 for all three models, with
every interval below zero, and has intervals that include zero at horizon 30; against \hc{} it runs from
-0.1182 to -0.1466 with every interval below zero; on the grid every scaled interval includes zero. The
oracle, a diagnostic, has the lowest mean \qs{} at both horizons and the lowest cell mean in 17 of the 18
cells, with a flat PIT histogram. Per cell, the largest differences against the hurdle are at $z = 0.2$ and
$\mu_{+} = 25$, and in the four cells with $z = 0.95$ and $\mu_{+}$ of 5 or 25, 23 of the 24 differences
favor the hurdle, all small beside those.

\paragraph{Post-hoc: the family without the three zero days.} This paragraph is post-hoc. It was computed
on 2026-10-16 from the saved npm forecasts, after the registered scores were seen, under item 6 of the
dated addition of 2026-10-16, and it replaces nothing above. Each removed day is dropped, for every series
and variant, from the one horizon-30 block that holds it, and the block is re-scored by the registered
formula over its remaining 29 days; the horizon-7 blocks hold none of the days; the registered aggregation,
test, seeded bootstrap and one adjustment over the eighteen then run unchanged (the same code reproduces the
registered tables byte for byte on the saved scores). Without all three days, which re-scores 10,032
forecasts, none of the eighteen conclusions changes: no row changes the side with the larger signed-rank sum,
the sign of its mean or its rejection, and twelve reject as registered, ten for the variant and two against
the hurdle. Without the two panel-wide days only, which re-scores 6,688 forecasts, one conclusion changes:
E06, Chronos-2's raw deciles against \lm{} at horizon 30, no longer rejects, with adjusted $p$ 0.0599 and
raw $p$ 0.0399 against 0.0119 and 0.00663 registered; it still favors the model by rank sum, with the model
lower on 110 of 209 series, and the other seventeen are unchanged, eleven rejecting. The horizon-7 rows are
identical except for their adjusted $p$, which moves because the adjustment ranks all eighteen together.
Without the three days the hurdle-against-control means at horizon 30 grow, E04 from 90.24 to 159.3, E10
from 128.5 to 201.2 and E16 from 110.3 to 183.4, and those means belong to \texttt{aggregate-error}. Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:s2sens} gives all eighteen rows under both removals. The mechanism answer does not depend on
the three days; the count of raw-against-local-mean rejections is the one figure that does, four of six as
registered and three of six without the two panel-wide days, with E06 the borderline row.

\paragraph{Read together.} The registered question has two parts and each is answered by its own contrasts.
Replacing a frozen model's own zero mass with the 28-day context-only occurrence probability, keeping the
model's completed positive law, does not lower \qs{}: on npm no cell favors the hurdle and two of six reject
against it; on the grid all three reject against it. The model's positive law beats a fixed count hurdle
with the same occurrence rule in all six npm cells, and loses or ties on the grid, where the generator's
positive law is the count hurdle's own family. The raw deciles beat the local mean on the npm mean in every
cell and on the rank test in four of six. Null, adverse, mixed and imprecise outcomes are reported with the
same care as a positive one.
