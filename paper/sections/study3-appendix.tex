\paragraph{The family.} Table~\ref{tab:s3structure} gives the registered order of the 36 tests and
Table~\ref{tab:s3family} the family with every column; Figure~\ref{fig:s3family} draws it. Twenty-one of
the thirty-six tests reject at $q = 0.05$ after the one adjustment: six are T1 tests in the dense stratum,
against the dynamic hurdle as H3 predicts; one is a T1 test in the sparse stratum, for the dynamic hurdle as
H3 predicts; and fourteen are T2 tests, all for the dynamic hurdle, three of them with a median of exactly
zero. No test rejects in a direction opposite to H3's prediction, and no T1 median has the opposite sign. The
direction of every test is read from the paired table, the median's sign and the rank sums of the absolute
nonzero differences, which reproduce every raw $p$ exactly (Table~\ref{tab:s3direction}); a median of exactly
zero has no sign under the registered rule, and the side the rank sums favor is a reading beside it.

\paragraph{The run.} The run started 2026-10-17 03:47 UTC at the implementation commit, after the signed
readiness and the gate (Appendix~\ref{app:s3detail}), and completed its 300 series in one invocation at
04:03 UTC, in 939 seconds inside the run and 956 for the whole process, with 39,600 forecasts (300 series, 3
origins, 2 horizons, 22 variants), no failed window and no resume, on the same NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070
SUPER (driver 610.88, CUDA 12.8, PyTorch 2.11.0) at batch size one, with a peak reserved memory of
2,948,595,712 bytes, from the locally cached weights with the offline variables set. The integrity check
verified all 300 checkpoint hashes, found every registered window exactly once, and recomputed \qs{}, its
three accounting parts and the context-scaled \qs{} for all 39,600 forecasts and the occurrence Brier score
for the 30,600 that carry a count law, from the saved deciles, targets and laws, with 0 disagreements; the
largest absolute residuals are 0.0 for \qs{} and the scaled score, $7.1 \times 10^{-15}$ for the parts and
$2.8 \times 10^{-16}$ for the Brier score. The structure the addition registers for the two new variants holds
on every target day of all 10,800 of their windows: \emph{hurdle\_dynamic}'s occurrence equals
\texttt{dynamic\_count}'s and its positive law is the completed law conditioned on $Y \ge 1$, and the fourth
cell's occurrence equals the completed law's with \hc{}'s positive law. The discrete CRPS is certified for
all 30,600 forecasts with a count law; the 9,000 without one have none by construction, and none is
uncertified. The inference ran from 04:03 to 04:04 UTC and the integrity check from 04:04 to 04:05 UTC,
both with HEAD at the implementation commit and no uncommitted change under the scripts or the package,
with NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1 and pandas 2.3.3.

exact ties, the nonzero differences, and the rank sums of the absolute nonzero series differences with
average ranks for ties, split by sign; the larger sum names the side the signed-rank test leans to. The raw
$p$-value recomputed from these differences, two-sided and approximate with the zeros omitted, equals the
saved one in every row.

\paragraph{The two readings of the accounting trade.} The hurdle's positive law $G_+$ is the model's completed
marginal law truncated at one, not a size forecast the model made for positive days; context zero mass laid
over a law that already sits low on positive days counts the zeros twice, and zero-day loss down with
underprediction up by more is what that predicts. Two readings of the net sign were open after Study 2: that
the context estimate is the worse occurrence forecast, and that the truncated law is not the model's size
forecast, so that any added zero mass hurts. The record supports the first on NYC 311's dense series, where
the dynamic occurrence is worse on the Brier score itself in all six rows and the fourth cell against the
count hurdle, which differs from it in the occurrence alone, puts the model's own occurrence ahead of the
Jeffreys estimate on 62 to 73 of the 100 dense series under a count law that cannot count zeros twice, and at
a week on the grid for Chronos-2 and TimesFM 3. It supports the second at a month on npm and the grid, where
the Jeffreys estimate is as good as or better than the model's zero mass on the Brier score, and on the grid
under the count law too, yet laid over the models' truncated positive laws it raises \qs{} against the control
on 57 to 66 of the 100 dense npm series and on 24 or 25 of the 30 grid blocks; under the count law on npm the
model's own zero mass stays ahead, lower on 43, 48 and 60 of the 100 dense series at a month with every mean
in its favor. The fourth cell against the control differs from it in the positive law alone and measures the
model's law against a shifted Poisson, far worse on dense NYC 311 series by 0.2944 to 0.325 on the mean; it
says nothing about either reading. The npm and grid attributions here are post hoc, resting on the
benchmark's fourth cell and the declared secondary Brier rows. Which reading operates depends on the panel
and the horizon, and nothing beyond that is attributed.
