\label{app:s3tables}

\paragraph{The run.} The run started 2026-10-17 03:47 UTC at the implementation commit, after the signed
readiness and the gate (Appendix~\ref{app:s3detail}), and completed its 300 series in one invocation at
04:03 UTC, in 939 seconds inside the run and 956 for the whole process, with 39,600 forecasts (300 series, 3
origins, 2 horizons, 22 variants), no failed window and no resume, on the same NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070
SUPER (driver 610.88, CUDA 12.8, PyTorch 2.11.0) at batch size one, with a peak reserved memory of
2,948,595,712 bytes, from the locally cached weights with the offline variables set. The integrity check
verified all 300 checkpoint hashes, found every registered window exactly once, and recomputed \qs{}, its
three accounting parts and the context-scaled \qs{} for all 39,600 forecasts and the occurrence Brier score
for the 30,600 that carry a count law, from the saved deciles, targets and laws, with 0 disagreements; the
largest absolute residuals are 0.0 for \qs{} and the scaled score, $7.1 \times 10^{-15}$ for the parts and
$2.8 \times 10^{-16}$ for the Brier score. The structure the addition registers for the two new variants holds
on every target day of all 10,800 of their windows: \emph{hurdle\_dynamic}'s occurrence equals
\texttt{dynamic\_count}'s and its positive law is the completed law conditioned on $Y \ge 1$, and the fourth
cell's occurrence equals the completed law's with \hc{}'s positive law. The discrete CRPS is certified for
all 30,600 forecasts with a count law; the 9,000 without one have none by construction, and none is
uncertified. The inference ran from 04:03 to 04:04 UTC and the integrity check from 04:04 to 04:05 UTC,
both with HEAD at the implementation commit and no uncommitted change under the scripts or the package,
with NumPy 2.5.3, SciPy 1.18.1 and pandas 2.3.3.
