## The design, in brief

The generator is the registered one: a stationary Markov Bernoulli occurrence chain with zero probability z
and persistence rho, times 1 + Poisson(mu - 1) positive sizes. The grid has 18 cells (z in 0.2, 0.8, 0.95;
mu in 1, 5, 25; rho in 0, 0.8) and 30 replicates per cell, each with 270 days. The origins are days 180, 210
and 240, and the horizons are 7 and 30. Common random numbers are shared within each replicate, and the root
is 20260918. All 16 registered model variants were forecast at every origin and horizon, and so was the oracle,
which uses the known parameters and the last observed state. The oracle is a diagnostic. It is not a
competitor and not part of the family.

How to read the tables:

- **Differences** are model minus reference QScore9. QScore9 is a loss, so a negative difference favors the
  hurdle variant and a positive one favors the reference.
- **The entity is the replicate block.** Origins are averaged within replicate and cell, then the 18 cells
  within replicate with equal weight, so n = 30.
- **Intervals** are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 20,000 replicate-block bootstrap replicates. The
  per-comparison seeds are in `inference/provenance.json`.
- **Raw p** is from a two-sided approximate Wilcoxon signed-rank test over the 30 block differences, with
  zero differences omitted. **BH p** applies Benjamini-Hochberg once over the six, at q = 0.05. The
  signed-rank test's location reading needs symmetry; it is not a test of the mean difference.
- **"Lower / tied / higher"** counts the blocks where the model's score is below, equal to or above the
  reference's.
- Inference is conditional on the generator and the fixed origins.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `run.json` | The run's state: identity, metadata (readiness, family, specification, implementation commit, source hashes, packages, hardware), the SHA-256 of each checkpoint, per-model timings, wall time, memory |
| `inference/` | The output of `scripts/study2_inference.py`: `inference.csv` and `inference.json` (the family), `paired_entities.csv`, `series_scores.csv`, `secondary_*.csv`, `pit_histograms.csv`, `pit_values.parquet`, `provenance.json` |
| `make_tables.py` | Prints the tables above from `inference/` |
| `recompute_scores.py`, `score-recompute.json` | The recomputation of every saved QScore9 and its result |
| `results-provenance.json` | The SHA-256 of every file in this directory, the checkpoints included, and of the inputs |
| `.gitattributes`, `.gitignore` | Byte preservation; the checkpoints are kept out of the tree |

The 540 whole-series checkpoints (`<SHA-256 of the series label>.json`, about 0.9 GB) are not in the tree,
just as Study 1's checkpoints are not. Their SHA-256 values are in `run.json`, `inference/provenance.json`
and `results-provenance.json`. The inference and the recomputation read them.

## Regeneration

From the repository root, PowerShell. The run and the gate pass only at the implementation commit, with the
smoke and readiness at the paths the readiness names (`data\raw\study2\`; the tracked copies are under
`study\study2\`). A completed run directory is immutable, so a rerun takes a new `--out`.

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\study2_smoke.py --gpu --context-days 1400 --leads 60 --out data\raw\study2\smoke-3fc3411.json
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\study2_gate.py synthetic --readiness data\raw\study2\synthetic-readiness-3fc3411.json
$env:HF_HUB_OFFLINE = "1"; $env:TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE = "1"; .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\study2_run.py synthetic --readiness data\raw\study2\synthetic-readiness-3fc3411.json --out reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\study2_inference.py synthetic --run reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15 --out <new directory>
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15\make_tables.py reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15\inference
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15\recompute_scores.py reports\study2-synthetic-2026-10-15 --out <new file>
```

blob at `3fc3411` (`fd32d317ab7770e35a872bf343b7ea683a942109002a3e757d748fe2eea1414c`), with NumPy 2.5.3,
SciPy 1.18.1 and pandas 2.3.3. HEAD was then `a73cdb1`, which differs from `3fc3411` only under `docs/`, with
no uncommitted change under the script or `pulsegraph/`. `inference/provenance.json` records all of this.
